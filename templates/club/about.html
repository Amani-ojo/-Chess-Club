About the club portal

Eschen Chess Club combines traditional over-the-board management with Celery-backed Lichess imports and Stockfish analysis.

Administrators record OTB results in Django admin; adaptive K-factor ELO updates run automatically when a match is marked complete. Online games stay on the Celery pathway so browsers never wait for Stockfish.

Staff MFA: enroll a TOTP device under your user in admin after upgrading this deployment.
