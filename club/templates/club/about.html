Our Story

The Eschen Chess Club brings together chess enthusiasts of all skill levels in the village of Eschen, Liechtenstein. Whether you are a seasoned player or just learning the game, our doors are open.

We meet regularly for friendly games, structured tournaments, and coaching sessions. Our ELO rating system tracks performance so members can see their progress over time.

Meeting Times

Tuesdays and Thursdays, 18:00 — 21:00

Location

Eschen Dorfplatz, 9492 Eschen, Liechtenstein

Membership

Membership is open to everyone. Contact us using the form or drop by during our meeting hours.
