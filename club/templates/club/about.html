About Us

Strategy, community, and decades of chess in Liechtenstein

Our Story

A village club with a serious board.

The Eschen Chess Club brings together chess enthusiasts of all skill levels in the village of Eschen, Liechtenstein. Whether you are a seasoned tournament player or have just learned the moves, our doors are open.

We meet regularly for friendly games, structured club tournaments, and coaching sessions. Our FIDE-style ELO rating system tracks every member's progress over time so you can watch your game evolve.
